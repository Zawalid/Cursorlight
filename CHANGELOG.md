# Changelog

All notable changes. Format follows Keep a Changelog.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Cursor highlight in five styles (Aura, Lens, Comet, Keyline, Sonar) with color, size, intensity and screen dim.
- Laser trail and Ink: draw on the screen, strokes fade, rough shapes straighten into ellipses, rectangles, lines, underlines and arrows.
- Triggers: shake, double-tap and hold a modifier key (optional Input Monitoring), global shortcuts, pause.
- Profiles (Stream, Meeting, Recording and your own), per-app rules and display scope.
- Share-safe reminder: tell Beacon what you share and it warns when viewers will not see effects.
- System cursor size, restored on quit and after a crash.
- Settings window, menu bar popover and a first-run guide.
