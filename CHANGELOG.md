## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added
- Highlight in six styles (Aura, Lens, Comet, Keyline, Sonar, Crosshair) with color, custom colors, size, intensity, one option per style, click effects (ripple, double ring, burst, pulse), an optional click sound, a hold focus ring, fading when the pointer is still and a locator ring when it jumps displays.
- Laser trail and Ink with five pens, eight colors plus custom, thickness, snapped shapes, arrows (straight, curved, double), brackets, boxes, a cover box, text labels, number and symbol stamps, a speech bubble, a whiteboard, an eraser, undo and persistent strokes.
- Spotlight: light a region (rectangle, rounded or ellipse) and dim the rest, with soft edges, while clicks still reach what is inside.
- Pointer skins: replace the real pointer with Classic, Dart, Bolt, Ring, Target, Star, Heart or Ghost, with color and size, saved per scene.
- Scenes with presets (Teaching, Webinar, Tutorial, Pairing, High visibility), reorder, duplicate, rename, export and import, per-app rules, app category rules and a default scene.
- Triggers: shake, double-tap and hold a modifier (optional Input Monitoring), rebindable global shortcuts with conflict checks, pause, next scene.
- Floating tool bar, presenter timer chip and a keystroke display with an optional key sound (optional Input Monitoring).
- `cursorlight://` links to control Cursorlight from Stream Deck, Raycast, Alfred, Shortcuts and scripts.
- Share-safe reminder: tell Cursorlight what you share and it warns when viewers will not see effects. The bar can hide from screen sharing.
- Hide automatically in full-screen apps and video players, and follow Reduce Motion.
- System cursor size, restored on quit and after a crash.
- Settings window with a live preview canvas, light and dark appearance.
- Menu bar popover with the tools, scenes, color and size, quick toggles (keys, clicks, bar, timer, board) and the share check, and a menu bar icon that shows ready, running and paused.
- A guided welcome that asks you to shake the mouse, press the laser key and draw, on your own screen, with hints drawn in the middle of the screen.
